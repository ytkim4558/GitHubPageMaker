# About

I am Yongtak Kim, an AWS Cloud Support Engineer and OpenSearch SME.

My work centers on customer troubleshooting, analytics workload support, and
turning repeated support engineering decisions into reliable AI-assisted
workflows. I started from mobile app and backend product development, then
expanded into image processing, data platforms, cloud analytics, and AI-agent
tooling.

## What I Work On

- OpenSearch customer issue analysis and troubleshooting
- Analytics support across AWS services
- Support-agent persona design and verification rules
- MCP-based workflows that connect Slack, Outlook, wiki knowledge, ticket search, and local tools
- Case aging triage with LLM summaries, missing-step detection, escalation readiness checks, and engineer/manager follow-up guidance
- Developer productivity tools for AI-agent session search, handoff, and documentation
